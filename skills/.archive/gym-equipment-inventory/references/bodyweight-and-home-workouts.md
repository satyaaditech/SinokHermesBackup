## 2. Core Movement Library for Home Sessions

1. **Bodyweight Squat / Box Squat**:
   - Target: Quads, glutes, hip mobility.
   - Cue: Sit back into hips like targeting an invisible chair; keep chest proud and heels anchored.
2. **Push-up / Incline Push-up**:
   - Target: Chest, anterior deltoids, triceps.
   - Cue: Hands slightly wider than shoulders; body in a solid rigid plank; elbows tucked at 45° (not flared at 90°). Use table/sofa elevation if shoulder/wrist relief is needed.
3. **Forearm Plank**:
   - Target: Transverse abdominis, deep core.
   - Cue: Elbows directly beneath shoulders; squeeze glutes and pull belly button to spine; avoid sagging hips.
4. **Glute Bridge**:
   - Target: Gluteus maximus, hamstrings, lower back relief.
   - Cue: Lie on back with knees bent; drive through heels to elevate hips until hips, knees, and shoulders form a straight line.
5. **Chair / Bench Dips**:
   - Target: Triceps and front shoulders.
   - Cue: Grip the edge of a sturdy chair; lower hips close to the chair edge until elbows reach 90°; push back up through palms.
