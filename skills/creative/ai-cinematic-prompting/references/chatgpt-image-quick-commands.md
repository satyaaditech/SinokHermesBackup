# 99 Viral AI Image Quick Commands & Modifiers

Categorized quick directives for ChatGPT (DALL-E 3), Google Gemini (Imagen 3), and Midjourney to rapidly inject specific cameras, lighting, aesthetics, and genres.

## Quick Reference by Category

### 1. Resolution & Realism Quality Modifiers (1-5)
* `/4k:` Ultra-high resolution render with clean details.
* `/8k:` Razor-sharp texture clarity, pores, micro-details.
* `/hdreal:` Hyper-realistic photographic lighting and authentic human skin tones.
* `/ultrarealistic:` Authentic skin subsurface scattering and lifelike physics.
* `/cinematic:` Hollywood feature film aesthetic, anamorphic aspect, 35mm look.

### 2. Camera Angles & Viewpoints (6-19)
* `/droneview:`, `/aerialview:`, `/topdown:` High elevation, geographic context, or flat lay.
* `/lowangle:`, `/wormseyeview:` Heroic, towering subject emphasis.
* `/highangle:`, `/birdseyeview:` Vulnerable, omniscient, or structural overview.
* `/sideview:`, `/closeup:`, `/extremecloseup:` Intimate details, macro textures, eye expressions.
* `/wideangle:`, `/fisheye:`, `/pov:` Expansive field of view or first-person immersion.
* `/overtheshoulder:` Subject engagement with environment or conversant.

### 3. Atmospheric Weather & Time (20-33)
* `/fog:`, `/mist:`, `/rainynight:`, `/rain:`, `/storm:`, `/snow:` Dynamic environmental elements.
* `/sunset:`, `/sunrise:`, `/goldenhour:`, `/bluehour:`, `/midnight:` Natural lighting color temperatures.
* `/neonnight:`, `/cyberpunk:`, `/cyber:` Vibrant cityscapes, artificial high-contrast hues.

### 4. Lighting, Optics & Studio Setups (34-44)
* `/softlighting:`, `/dramaticlighting:`, `/rimlight:`, `/backlight:` Key lighting styles.
* `/volumetriclight:`, `/godrays:` Atmospheric light beams cutting through particulate.
* `/studio:`, `/filmgrain:`, `/anamorphic:`, `/bokeh:`, `/shallowdepth:` Optical characteristics.

### 5. Dynamic Shutter & Photography Styles (45-60)
* `/motionblur:`, `/longexposure:`, `/freezeaction:` Shutter speed dynamics.
* `/vintage:`, `/retro:`, `/oldmoney:`, `/luxury:`, `/minimalist:`, `/editorial:`, `/fashion:`, `/streetstyle:`, `/magazinecover:` Aesthetic genres.
* `/polaroid:`, `/disposablecamera:`, `/35mmfilm:`, `/instantphoto:` Emulsion chemistry looks.

### 6. Environments & Biomes (61-68)
* `/underwater:`, `/space:`, `/desert:`, `/forest:`, `/mountains:`, `/beach:`, `/cityscape:`, `/nightcity:`

### 7. Thematic & Cinematic Genres (69-77)
* `/futuristic:`, `/postapocalyptic:`, `/fantasy:`, `/scifi:`, `/medieval:`, `/samurai:`, `/noir:`, `/detective:`, `/superhero:`

### 8. Art Styles & Dimensional Mediums (78-88)
* `/anime`, `/comicbook`, `/oilpainting`, `/watercolor`, `/sketch`, `/claystyle`, `/3drender`, `/isometric`, `/miniature`, `/tiltshift`, `/papercraft`

### 9. Visual FX, Textures & Surreal Aesthetics (89-99)
* `/glassart:`, `/chrome:`, `/holographic:`, `/doubleexposure:`, `/silhouette:`, `/reflection:`, `/mirror:`, `/fire:`, `/smoke:`, `/glitch:`, `/dreamcore:`

## Stacking Formula
`[Subject & Action] + [Lighting & Weather] + [Camera Angle] + [Photography Style / Resolution Modifiers]`
* Example: `A ronin warrior standing in the rain. /cinematic /samurai /cyberpunk /rainynight /volumetriclight /anamorphic /8k`
