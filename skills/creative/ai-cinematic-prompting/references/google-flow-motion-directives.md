# Google Flow & Veo Video Motion Directives (Part 02)

High-converting motion directives, viral opening hooks, and VFX transitions for Google Flow, Veo (VideoFX), Runway Gen-3, and Kling AI.

## Key Directive Categories

### 1. Scroll-Stopping Viral Hooks (0-3s Opening)
* `/scrollstopper:` Sudden dramatic subject/object movement stopping sharply before camera lens.
* `/patterninterrupt:` Unexpected physics or spatial disruption (e.g. gravity inversion).
* `/curiosityhook:` Dolly into glowing mystery entrance or locked box.
* `/shockreveal:` Natural-looking static statue blinking or shifting gaze.
* `/loopbait:` Camera returns seamlessly to exact starting keyframe.

### 2. UGC & Creator Content
* `/ugc:` Authentic handheld feel with natural lighting.
* `/selfiecam:` Front-facing mobile vlog angle with organic micro-shake.
* `/talkinghead:` Direct eye contact with subtle expressive gestures.
* `/creatorstudio:` Premium studio desk setup with atmospheric backlights.

### 3. Commercial & Product Hero
* `/producthero:` Product locked at center stage with majestic entrance.
* `/beautyshot:` Flawless 360 rotation with glistening specular highlights.
* `/texture:` Macro focus on fabric weaves, metal brushing, or water droplets.

### 4. Impossible AI VFX
* `/impossibletransition:` Camera glides effortlessly through solid glass/mirrors/walls.
* `/dimensionjump:` Seamless shift between 2D sketch and 3D photorealistic reality.
* `/levitationfx:` Physics-defying weightless suspension of subject/objects.

## Stacking Formula
Combine keyframe description with 2-4 motion directives:
`[Keyframe Image] -> /scrollstopper: /beautyshot: /impossibletransition: [Active motion path, particle dynamics, 24fps].`
